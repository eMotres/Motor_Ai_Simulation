## Follow-up feasibility check

`tests/test_static3d_band2d.py` obtains its `MotorSection`, `BandedSection` and winding from the saved preset, CAD geometry, gmsh meshing and phase-winding builder. The new `tests/test_torque_virtual_work_gate.py` instead constructs an explicit torn 90-degree annular sector: duplicate rotor/stator ring vertices, paired radial-cut nodes and P2 facets, one tagged higher-permeability rotor patch, and a stationary sinusoidal winding source. It executes the real `Banded2D` P2 projection, linear solve and coenergy at shifts -2…2 with `nu_pointwise=True`. No configuration, CAD, gmsh or live API enters the fixture.

With 12 sector cells, one- and two-step central differences give 1.2762442020650024e-5 and 1.105259900420861e-5 N m. With 24 cells, they give 1.323864496243016e-5 and 1.2787549074286104e-5 N m. The step-size spread falls from 1.71e-6 to 4.51e-7 N m. A second perturbation holds the weld fixed and advances the stationary source phase by the equivalent relative angle. Its torque is 1.2762442020649662e-5 and 1.323864496243223e-5 N m on the respective meshes, agreeing with the weld-shift result to roundoff. The sign follows from `sin(2θ + phase)`: increasing phase rotates the winding backward, the same relative motion as advancing the rotor. This is an independent source-rotation route through the same linear discrete energy model; exact agreement tests rotational covariance of this constructed mesh and source, not continuum accuracy. Reversing the winding current leaves this pure reluctance torque unchanged to the asserted relative 1e-10. At each shift, the linear magnetic coenergy agrees with the independently assembled half source-field pairing to relative 2e-9. No production motor validation follows from this toy annulus. The project Python 3.11 interpreter imports `skfem`, SciPy and Shapely; the bundled workspace interpreter does not have `skfem`.

The nonlinear extension uses an explicit-origin, monotone B-H table for the rotor patch: `(H A/m, B T)` = `(0,0), (3000,.02), (9000,.05), (25000,.10), (100000,.20)`. Every solve in the 12-cell test converged with pointwise quadrature and constitutive residual below 1e-6. At the zero shift, the independently assembled source-work derivative is `2 * volume_factor * A · (df/dphase)` = 1.1266528136197504e-5 N m. A small fixed-weld source-phase difference gives 1.1266523998465505e-5 N m (relative difference 3.7e-7). At the full one-cell angle, weld-shift coenergy gives 1.0733567119524323e-5 N m and an equivalent fixed-weld source-phase displacement gives 1.0733567119522407e-5 N m. The 4.73% gap between the point derivative and the full-cell central difference is finite-angle curvature; neither value was rescaled or treated as a production reference. The source-work identity follows from the stationary nonlinear magnetic functional: at fixed current, `dW'/dphase = A · df/dphase`, with the factor two converting electrical source phase to mechanical angle. This table has an explicit origin so its interpolation is consistent in both the solver and coenergy routine; this test does not establish that every stored material card has that property. The nonlinear diagnostic still does not validate continuum torque, a real motor, PM cogging, eddy losses or irreversible state.
