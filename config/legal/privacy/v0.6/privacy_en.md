# AeroStator Privacy Policy

Version {{version}}. This policy explains how MOTRES d.o.o. processes personal data in AeroStator (aerostator.com). The English text governs; the Chinese text is a full mirror.

MOTRES d.o.o., Kotnikova 34, 1000 Ljubljana, Slovenia (EU), registration no. 7255691000, VAT SI22399933. Contact: support@aerostator.com. For personal data in a client's projects that MOTRES processes on the client's instructions, the client is the controller and MOTRES its processor, or subprocessor where the client is itself a processor (Platform Agreement, Schedule 1). MOTRES is the controller for its own purposes listed in section 3, including the screening and removal of personal data from engineering data before AI training.

## 2. Data we process

(a) Account data: name, business e-mail address, company and job title where given, password hash or Google sign-in identifier, sessions, API and agent keys. (b) Project and workspace data you create, including names in documents and file metadata; project-contact data may also come from the client or another authorised contributor to a project. (c) Acceptance records: accepted Terms, Platform Agreement and NDA versions, the hashes of the texts shown, interface language, the country, business name and capacity you state, the applicable dispute clause, mode, the optional choice on earlier data, signatory, company, time, IP address and browser. (d) Per-user active working time in the app, shown in MOTRES's administrator view. (e) Communication preferences and their consent history. (f) Support and help-chat messages, verification requests and quote requests. (g) Technical and security logs: IP address, browser, session identifiers, authentication and agent-tool events.

Required fields are marked when we collect them; without them the account or feature you ask for cannot be provided. Optional communication and publication choices do not affect account access.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR Art. 6(1))

- Providing the account, simulations, projects, collaboration, NDA access and support: contract with you where you are the contracting party (b); for employees and other users acting for a business, MOTRES's and that business's legitimate interest in providing the service (f).
- Preparing engineering data for MOTRES's internal AI training, limited to automated detection and removal of personal data before data enter a training dataset: MOTRES as controller, legitimate interest in developing its engineering models (f). No personal data is used to train or evaluate a model. You may object (section 9); MOTRES restricts this processing while it assesses the objection and stops it unless compelling legitimate grounds are established.
- Acceptance records and legal obligations: legal obligation (c) and legitimate interest in proving agreements (f).
- Security, abuse prevention, cookieless statistics and the administrator's activity view: legitimate interest in operating and securing the platform (f). Active-time records are used only for service administration, support and abuse investigation, are visible only to authorised MOTRES administrators and are not used for automated decisions about anyone's employment.
- Google Analytics cookies and optional e-mail topics: your consent (a), which you can withdraw at any time.
- Records of your community-dataset choice: legitimate interest in demonstrating and respecting data permissions (f). That choice covers non-personal engineering data only; the community dataset contains no personal data.

## 4. AI training

In FREE mode MOTRES trains its own internal AI models on engineering data (Terms of Use section 3). Personal data is not used for that: before data enter a training dataset, names, e-mail addresses, company names, names in file metadata, file paths, embedded content, free-text messages and comments, assistant or agent conversation records and other indirect identifiers are removed or excluded, and data that cannot be reliably cleaned are excluded. The account and project tags needed to remove records later are kept in a separate restricted register and are not training inputs. Only accounts and companies that accepted FREE mode are used, only for data supplied after acceptance unless the optional box for earlier data was ticked. Data in PRIVATE mode are never used for AI training.

## 5. Recipients and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal data. Personal data is processed by the providers listed in Schedule 2 of the Platform Agreement: Hetzner Online GmbH (hosting and backups, EU); Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC (Workspace e-mail and storage, optional Google sign-in, Google Analytics only after consent); Anthropic, PBC (the optional help, specification and design assistants, which are AI systems, and machine translation of production texts); Anthropic, OpenAI and Google AI subscription accounts used by MOTRES staff on a client's project, with the use of submitted content for model training switched off in each account; shipping partners for physical deliveries; and accounting or legal advisers under confidentiality. Providers receiving project or assistant content through the Platform are contractually prohibited from using it for their own model training or service improvement; in the staff AI subscription accounts this use is switched off in the account settings. Processing associated with your own Google account, when you sign in with Google, is governed by Google's own privacy notice.

## 6. International transfers

The Platform's servers are in Germany and its backups in the EU. Some providers are in the United States; transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request a copy of the applicable safeguards at support@aerostator.com.

## 7. Analytics and cookies

Self-hosted Umami statistics are cookieless and collect no personal identifiers. Google Analytics runs only after you consent in the cookie banner, and never for visitors from Mainland China, Hong Kong or Macau, in Chinese interface mode, or with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control enabled. You can change your choice at any time in Cookie settings.

## 8. Retention

Account and workspace data are kept while the account is active. A deletion request from the account menu takes effect after a 7-day grace period in which you can cancel; training use of the affected data stops when the request is received and verified, and the data are then removed from active systems. Development-project and production data are deleted under Platform Agreement section 11.5, and MOTRES instructs its providers to delete their copies. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 12 months (hourly for 24 hours, daily for 30 days, monthly for 12 months), and deletions are re-applied to restored data before they are used again. Active-time records are kept while the account is active and deleted with it. Security logs are kept only as long as needed to investigate security incidents and abuse, with pseudonymised identifiers after account deletion. Training datasets contain no personal data; records are removed from them under Platform Agreement section 11.1. Acceptance and NDA records are kept for as long as claims under the agreements are possible. Enquiries are kept up to 3 years after the last contact. Invoices are generally retained for ten years after the end of the year to which they relate under Article 86 of Slovenia's VAT Act (ZDDV-1), with any longer legally required period applied to the relevant records.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time (e-mail topics at /preferences, Cookie settings). You can withdraw a community-dataset choice in the account menu. You can download your data and request account deletion in the account menu, or write to support@aerostator.com. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (ip-rs.si).

## 10. Security

We use HTTPS, password hashing, scoped and revocable tokens, project-based access control, audited administrator access and encrypted backups, and we regularly test these measures. No transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## 11. Changes

We publish changes on this page with a new version number. Changes that need your consent or acceptance are asked for separately.
