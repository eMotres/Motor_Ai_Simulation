Computed (2-D, cold 20 °C coil and magnet unless noted):

- Rated cold point: Kt 0.217 N·m/A rms, Km 0.5018 N·m/√W (554.8 s solve).
- Static MTPA rows at 6.47, 12.94, 19.41, 25.88 and 38.82 A rms.

  25.88 A, 1000 rpm: 5.6622 N·m, within 0.0002 % of the fitted vertex.

  0.925 N·m (FW35 rejected).

  Ld/Lq ≈ 0.141 / 0.144 mH and Kt₀ 0.2235 / Km₀ 0.5169 are unqualified
