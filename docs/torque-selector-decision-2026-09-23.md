# Decision gate for the 1 A torque selector — 2026-09-23

**Decision: the hard 1 A threshold is disproven as a physical eligibility rule, but do not replace the production selector yet.** `hybrid_torque` in `sb_postproc.py` selects a fundamental space-vector mean when *peak per-branch* current exceeds 1 A and usable phase-linkage arrays exist; otherwise it returns raw Maxwell torque. It keeps Maxwell's AC component when it substitutes the mean. The saved GEO30 runs measure a selected-mean drop of 0.000081312055 N·m between 1.000 and 1.001 A peak per branch while raw Maxwell and the terminal candidate both rise by about 0.0000051 N·m. The analytic fifth-spatial-harmonic case gives 2.31 N·m by fixed-current coenergy derivative versus 2.058 N·m from the fundamental space-vector helper, so that helper is not generally eligible. Selecting Maxwell everywhere is also unsupported by a general error bound.

The 60 A RMS run15/run16 imposed-current case gives an informative *one-point* check on the same 12-slot/14-pole mesh: cubic terminal path work 0.409648779 N·m, hybrid mean 0.409647190 N·m, raw Maxwell mean 0.408199784 N·m, and the mean of 48 independent ±one-slip-cell frozen-current coenergy secants 0.409501898 N·m. Corrected terminal linkage matches its winding source to 2.17e−19 Wb, and the accepted P2 material/PM energy law was reconstructed at quadrature. The virtual-work mean is 0.000146881 N·m below terminal path work; the integer-weld finite-displacement and mesh errors have no bound. A pure fundamental's ±cell secant has factor 0.999682720, but that is not a correction for the motor's harmonics. This result supports scale and sign at this operating point, not a universal selector or a low-current claim.

At 1 A peak per branch, the new run18 saved 96 converged states for 48 fixed-current ±one-slip-cell pairs. Its [offline review](../scripts/torque_frozen_lowcurrent_review.py) retains every secant and gives mean **0.005138892113 N·m**, compared with all-bin terminal spectral work **0.005140465326 N·m** and raw Maxwell **0.005226917746 N·m**. The secant is 0.000001573213 N·m below terminal work and 0.000088025633 N·m below Maxwell. Source–linkage reciprocity is within 1.63e−19 Wb and the reconstructed accepted nonlinear/PM energy law agrees numerically with production H. Run18 pinned the `d785a4c` `field_ops.py` Git blob and LF content; run15's mixed-EOL working-tree bytes were not archived, so byte identity across those runs is unproven. Both comparisons support all-bin terminal work for these **conservative, imposed-current, integer-period** cases, but do not bound integer-weld, spatial or window errors in general.

| Regime / candidate | Acceptance evidence needed before selection | Current decision |
|---|---|---|
| Imposed current, full integer-period window, no eddy/demag | Proven reciprocal phase linkage; accepted conservative material/PM functional; discrete moving-weld virtual-work/port-energy identity; actual angle and signed period metadata; angular and spatial error bounds; independent mean comparison at the relevant current. An explicit validated endpoint `ΔU` may replace exact periodic closure, but the present PM-inclusive potential difference is only arithmetic. | All-bin terminal work is supported by the 1 A and 60 A mean comparisons. Keep diagnostic-only until the identity/window/error budget is established. |
| Fundamental space-vector mean | Demonstrate that winding/coenergy rotor-position dependence has no material omitted harmonic/cogging mean over the tested regime. Check against independent virtual work over amplitude and angle; do not infer eligibility from sinusoidal current or peak amplitude. | Not generally eligible: analytic fifth-harmonic counterexample. |
| Zero current / no-load PM cogging | Independently converge local cogging torque and its period mean with appropriate spatial symmetry; terminal `Σi dψ` is identically zero and gives no information about cogging ripple. | Preserve raw Maxwell as a *candidate* with method/uncertainty visible; do not substitute zero or dq. |
| Low current, including peak branch 0.5, 1.000, 1.001 and 2 A | Use the same winding/material/mesh/angle provenance as the loaded case; compare raw Maxwell, all-bin terminal work and conservative virtual-work means. Judge absolute N·m error near zero rather than only percentage. Verify parallel-branch scaling with the actual effective path count. | Cases 0–3 establish the selector jump; run18 provides an independent fixed-current comparison at 1 A. Case 4 (2 A) and other low-current virtual-work points remain untested. |
| Noninteger, clipped or nonuniform angle window | Either a separately validated open-path identity with explicit endpoint magnetic-state correction and signed angles, or report only raw candidates. Do not close a noninteger trajectory artificially. | Existing periodic helper rejects; no production promotion. |
| Eddy, voltage/PWM, demagnetization or changing material | Include electrical/circuit and dissipative ports plus state evolution; a conservative imposed-current path integral is insufficient. | Ineligible for the present terminal-work diagnostic; leave selector behavior unchanged pending a separate method. |
| Sector symmetry | Use only winding/material/excitation-admissible sectors. GEO30 12s/14p permits NS1/NS2, not NS4. The separate G150 24s/28p NS1/2/4 five-angle check agrees locally within 0.15%, but does not certify full-period mean or ripple. | Sector agreement cannot authorize a torque formula or physics filtering. |

The next production design is an **eligibility-based all-bin terminal-work mean**, with an explicit reason when window or mode eligibility fails; raw Maxwell AC can be retained only after a separate ripple check. Before replacing the selected output, require signed, uniform, complete integer-period sampling (or an independently validated endpoint-energy correction), conservative imposed-current mode, reciprocal linkage and source, and a quantitative error budget against moving-weld virtual work over relevant currents, harmonics and admissible sectors. Noninteger/clipped windows, eddy, voltage/PWM, demagnetization and zero-current cogging need separate handling. No selector edit has been made; the available 1 A and 60 A mean checks do not certify the general method or AC ripple.

The bounded characterization produced cases **0, 0.5, 1.000 and 1.001 A peak per branch** on the same GEO30 NS2 mesh and 48-angle grid; case 4 at 2 A was deferred. Case 3's raw data converged, but an unrelated on-disk `sb_postproc.py` metadata edit after process import caused its end-of-run hash postcheck to fail; its pre-run source was identical to the earlier cases. Run18 subsequently supplied the 1 A fixed-current comparison. The [low-current note](torque-low-current-threshold-2026-09-23.md) records the full table and provenance limits.
