# Decision gate for the 1 A torque selector — 2026-09-23

**Decision: do not replace the production selector yet.** `hybrid_torque` in `sb_postproc.py` selects a fundamental space-vector mean when *peak per-branch* current exceeds 1 A and usable phase-linkage arrays exist; otherwise it returns raw Maxwell torque. It always keeps Maxwell's AC component when it substitutes the mean. The threshold has no physical eligibility meaning and creates a discontinuity, but deleting it would promote an unvalidated candidate across modes. The analytic fifth-spatial-harmonic case gives 2.31 N·m by fixed-current coenergy derivative versus 2.058 N·m from the space-vector helper. Selecting Maxwell everywhere is equally unsupported by a general error bound.

The 60 A RMS run15/run16 imposed-current case gives an informative *one-point* check on the same 12-slot/14-pole mesh: cubic terminal path work 0.409648779 N·m, hybrid mean 0.409647190 N·m, raw Maxwell mean 0.408199784 N·m, and the mean of 48 independent ±one-slip-cell frozen-current coenergy secants 0.409501898 N·m. Corrected terminal linkage matches its winding source to 2.17e−19 Wb, and the accepted P2 material/PM energy law was reconstructed at quadrature. The virtual-work mean is 0.000146881 N·m below terminal path work; the integer-weld finite-displacement and mesh errors have no bound. A pure fundamental's ±cell secant has factor 0.999682720, but that is not a correction for the motor's harmonics. This result supports scale and sign at this operating point, not a universal selector or a low-current claim.

The separate G150 24s/28p NS1/2/4 five-angle check agrees locally within 0.15%, but does not certify full-period mean or ripple. | Sector agreement cannot authorize a torque formula or physics filtering. |

The eventual selector should be **eligibility-based**, with a certified method and explicit reason when eligibility fails, rather than current-amplitude based. Its mean and AC/ripple choices need separate validation: preserving raw Maxwell AC does not make ripple converged. Before replacing the existing selected output, fix an application-level absolute/relative torque error budget and show the candidate satisfies it against independent virtual work across zero, low and loaded current, spatial harmonics, nonlinear material and allowed symmetry. No such budget or evidence exists today, so the defensible production action now is additive diagnostics and honest method labels, not a numerical switch.

A bounded **characterization** campaign, if authorized later, is five isolated current-drive, one-electrical-period GEO30 NS2 runs at *per-branch peak* currents **0, 0.5, 1.000, 1.001 and 2.0 A** (`I_phase_rms` respectively `0, 0.3535533906, 0.7071067812, 0.7078138879 and 1.4142135624 A` for `n_parallel=1`). Freeze run15's explicit geometry/materials, mesh 1.4/0.35 mm, 144 slip cells/electrical period, 48 snapped angles, 15,000 rpm, no eddy/demag, and one thread BelowNormal. Hard-cap each at 45 s and stop on failure, giving at most 225 s total; no repeat or sweep. Save **unfiltered** phase currents/linkages, actual angles, raw Maxwell/selected means and waveforms, method label, effective parallel count, convergence/residuals, mesh/solver/material hashes, and selected full P2 states. This detects the real motor's threshold jump and low-current candidate gap on a fixed discretization; because it lacks independent low-current virtual work and an endpoint, it **cannot alone authorize** a replacement. A subsequent small fixed-current displacement check would be required if these gaps justify it. No campaign was run for this decision.
