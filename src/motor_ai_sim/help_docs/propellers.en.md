# Simulations > Propellers

What it is for: all propeller work in one page. Staff with the simulation UI.

Two sub-tabs at the top: Catalogue and Designer.
- Catalogue: the known propellers, read-only, as a table (see the document for the Catalogue).
- Designer: your own propeller, evaluated with a blade-element model (see the document for the Designer).

The Thermal tab can use an assigned propeller's slipstream for cooling; Configure keeps its own propeller picker.

Everything in the Designer is a screening estimate: the banner "BEMT, generic airfoil polars, not validated" is always shown.
