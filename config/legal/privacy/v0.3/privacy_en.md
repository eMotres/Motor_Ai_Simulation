# AeroStator Privacy Policy

Version {{version}}. This policy explains how MOTRES d.o.o. processes personal data in AeroStator (aerostator.com). The English text governs; the Chinese text is a full mirror.

## 1. Controller

MOTRES d.o.o., Kotnikova 34, 1000 Ljubljana, Slovenia (EU), registration no. 7255691000, VAT SI22399933. Contact: support@aerostator.com. For personal data in a client's projects that MOTRES processes on the client's instructions, the client is the controller and MOTRES its processor (Platform Agreement, Schedule 1).

## 2. Data we process

(a) Account data: name, business e-mail address, company and job title where given, password hash or Google sign-in identifier, sessions, API and agent keys. (b) Project and workspace data you create, including names in documents and file metadata. (c) Acceptance records: accepted Terms, Platform Agreement and NDA versions, mode, signatory, company, time, IP address, browser and text hash. (d) Per-user active working time in the app, shown in the administrator view. (e) Communication preferences and their consent history. (f) Support and help-chat messages, verification requests and quote requests. (g) Technical and security logs: IP address, browser, session identifiers, authentication and agent-tool events.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR Art. 6(1))

- Providing the account, simulations, projects, collaboration, NDA access and support: contract with you where you are the contracting party (b); for employees and other users acting for a business, MOTRES's and that business's legitimate interest in providing the service (f).
- Preparing engineering data for MOTRES's internal AI training, including automated detection and removal of personal data before data enter a training dataset: MOTRES as controller, legitimate interest in developing its engineering models (f); you may object (section 9).
- Acceptance records, invoices and legal obligations: legal obligation (c) and legitimate interest in proving agreements (f).
- Security, abuse prevention, cookieless statistics and the administrator's activity view: legitimate interest in operating and securing the platform (f).
- Google Analytics cookies, optional e-mail topics and the optional community-dataset sharing: your consent (a), which you can withdraw at any time.

## 4. AI training

In FREE mode MOTRES trains its own internal AI models on engineering data (Terms of Use section 3). Personal data is not used for that: names, e-mail addresses, company names, names in file metadata, free-text messages and comments, and assistant or agent conversation records are removed or excluded before data enter a training dataset, and data that cannot be reliably cleaned are excluded. Only accounts and companies that accepted FREE mode are used. Data in PRIVATE mode are never used for AI training.

## 5. Recipients and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal data. Personal data is processed by the providers listed in Schedule 2 of the Platform Agreement: Hetzner Online GmbH (hosting and backups, EU); Google (Workspace e-mail and storage, optional Google sign-in, Google Analytics only after consent); Anthropic (the help, specification and design assistants, which are AI systems); OpenRouter (machine translation of production texts); Anthropic, OpenAI and Google (engineering tools used by MOTRES staff on a client's project); shipping partners for physical deliveries; and accounting or legal advisers under confidentiality. None of them may use your data to train their models.

## 6. International transfers

The Platform is hosted in Germany (EU). Some providers are in the United States; transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. Analytics and cookies

Self-hosted Umami statistics are cookieless and collect no personal identifiers. Google Analytics runs only after you consent in the cookie banner, and never for visitors from Mainland China, Hong Kong or Macau, in Chinese interface mode, or with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control enabled. You can change your choice at any time in Cookie settings.

## 8. Retention

Account and workspace data are kept while the account is active. A deletion request from the account menu takes effect after a 7-day grace period in which you can cancel; the data are then removed from active systems. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 12 months (hourly for 24 hours, daily for 30 days, monthly for 12 months), and deletions are re-applied after any restore. Security logs are kept with pseudonymised identifiers. Training datasets contain no personal data; records are removed from them under Platform Agreement section 11.1. Acceptance and NDA records are kept for as long as claims under the agreements are possible. Enquiries are kept up to 3 years after the last contact and invoices 10 years under Slovenian accounting law.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time (e-mail topics at /preferences, community-dataset sharing in the account menu, Cookie settings). You can download your data and request account deletion in the account menu, or write to support@aerostator.com. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (ip-rs.si).

## 10. Security

We use HTTPS, password hashing, scoped and revocable tokens, project-based access control, audited administrator access and encrypted backups. No transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## 11. Changes

We publish changes on this page with a new version number. Changes that need your consent or acceptance are asked for separately.
