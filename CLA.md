# MOTRES d.o.o. Contributor License Agreement

**CLA v1.0, effective on the date it is merged into this repository.**

This document contains two agreements:

- **Part A — Individual Contributor License Agreement** (you contribute on your
  own behalf);
- **Part B — Entity Contributor License Agreement** (you contribute on behalf
  of, or as an employee of, a company or other legal entity).

You accept the applicable agreement by signing it through the CLA Assistant
check on your first pull request (comment
`I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA`). One signature covers
all your later Contributions to MOTRES d.o.o. projects that reference this CLA.
