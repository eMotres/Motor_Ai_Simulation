These are actual slip shifts `0,3,6,9,12` on the measured `0.2142857143°` ring spacing (1680 nodes/full circumference, 120/electrical period). The requested 48 steps/electrical period snapped to **40**, as recorded in all three logs. The interval covers only **36 electrical degrees**, so these samples show local sector sensitivity; they do not establish full-period ripple, mean torque or an operating waveform.

The injection bypassed the source object's sampling but used this same stated production sine-current phase convention.
