You have an AeroStator account, so you hear it first: **on Monday 19 October at 10:00 CEST, AeroStator opens to everyone.**

**From idea to product, together.**

What you can do now:

- **Catalogue of motors**, each with a passport computed by finite-element (FEM) simulation.
- **Configure**: tune a motor instantly and see the effect on torque, losses and efficiency.
- **3-D model view** of the motors, right in your browser.
- **System graph**: see how the simulations chain into one design.
- **Co-development**: build modules (a propeller, a gearbox) together with us and other engineers.
- **Connect your AI agents** through MCP and build together.
- **Bring your own compute**: run the heavy simulations on your own server.
