# Schedules to the AeroStator Platform Services and Data Agreement

Version {{version}}. These Schedules form part of the AeroStator Platform Services and Data Agreement (the "Agreement"). Defined terms have the meaning given in the Agreement. The English text governs; the Chinese text is a full mirror.

## Schedule 1 — Data Processing Addendum

S1.1 **Scope and roles.** This Schedule applies where MOTRES processes Personal Data on the Client's behalf in providing project and collaboration functions (Agreement section 8.5). The Client is controller, or processor for its own client, and MOTRES is processor or subprocessor according to the Client's actual role. For account administration, fraud prevention, security and legal compliance MOTRES is an independent controller under the Privacy Notice, and this Schedule does not apply to that processing.

S1.2 **Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose.** Hosting, storage, computation, display, sharing with Authorised Recipients, support and backup of project data for the term of the Agreement and the deletion periods in Agreement section 11. No Personal Data is used for AI training (Agreement section 7.3). The screening of eligible FREE material and the removal of Personal Data from it before training are carried out by MOTRES as controller for that limited purpose, as authorised in Agreement section 8.5 and described in the Privacy Notice.

S1.3 **Data subjects and data.** Users and Authorised Recipients, and persons named in project data: names, business e-mail addresses, company and job title, account and access records, messages and comments in projects, and names in documents or file metadata. The Client shall not upload special categories of personal data or data on criminal convictions.

S1.4 **Instructions.** MOTRES processes the Personal Data only on the Client's documented instructions, including with regard to transfers of Personal Data to a third country, which are the Agreement, the Client's use and settings of the Platform, and further written instructions within its scope, unless EU or Member State law requires otherwise; MOTRES then informs the Client unless the law prohibits it. The Client determines the purposes and gives lawful instructions, provides the notices required towards data subjects, and may exercise the rights in this Schedule. MOTRES immediately informs the Client if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data-protection law.

S1.5 **Confidentiality of personnel.** Persons authorised to process the Personal Data are bound by confidentiality.

S1.6 **Security measures.** MOTRES applies the following measures and keeps them under review: HTTPS (TLS) for all access to the Platform; password hashing and scoped, revocable session, API and agent tokens; role- and project-based access control with logical separation of workspaces and projects; audited administrator access; least-privilege staff access; hosting in EU data centres; hourly encrypted backups (restic) to a separate EU storage service with the rotation hourly for 24 hours, daily for 30 days and monthly for 12 months; a register of deletions and training cut-offs that is re-applied to restored data before they become accessible to normal operations; security logging with pseudonymised identifiers after account deletion. MOTRES regularly tests and evaluates the effectiveness of its security and recovery measures. MOTRES does not claim certifications it does not hold.

S1.7 **Subprocessors.** The Client gives general written authorisation to the subprocessors in Schedule 2 that process Personal Data on its behalf. Agreement section 8.3 governs notice of additions or replacements and objections. MOTRES imposes on each subprocessor data-protection obligations equivalent to this Schedule and remains liable for their performance as required by law.

S1.8 **Assistance.** Taking into account the nature of the processing, MOTRES assists the Client by appropriate measures in responding to data-subject requests, and with security, breach notification, data-protection impact assessments and prior consultation, using the information available to it.

S1.9 **Personal-data breaches.** MOTRES notifies the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting the Client's Personal Data, with the information then available, and supplements it as further information becomes available.

S1.10 **Return and deletion.** At the end of the services, at the Client's choice, MOTRES returns the Personal Data (by export under Schedule 3) or deletes it, and deletes existing copies within the periods of Agreement section 11.5, unless Union or Member State law requires storage. Copies in routine backups are isolated, unavailable for any other use and deleted on expiry of the backup rotation; MOTRES keeps that residual period under review against its erasure duties and applies any shorter mandatory deadline, including under Agreement section 11.7.

S1.11 **Audit and information.** MOTRES makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Schedule and allows for and contributes to audits, including inspections, by the Client or an auditor mandated by it and bound by confidentiality. Routine audits take place at most once a year, on reasonable notice, during business hours and at the Client's cost; additional audits are permitted where reasonably necessary to verify compliance, including after a breach or at a regulator's request, and urgent justified audits are accommodated. Notice, timing and cost arrangements do not defeat statutory audit rights, and audits protect other clients' data.

S1.12 **International transfers.**
(a) *Transfers to the Client.* Where the Client is established outside the EEA in a country without an adequacy decision and its processing is not itself subject to the GDPR, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) are incorporated by reference between MOTRES as data exporter and the Client as data importer: Module 4 (processor to controller) where the Client is controller, and Module 3 (processor to processor) where the Client is itself a processor. The Clauses apply unmodified.
(b) *Options and annexes.* Clause 7 (docking) does not apply; under Clause 9(a), where applicable, Option 2 (general written authorisation, notice under Agreement section 8.3) applies; the optional wording of Clause 11 does not apply. Annex I is completed by the acceptance record (parties, contact details, signature and date), S1.2–S1.3 (categories, nature and purpose) and this S1.12 (continuous transfer for the term of the Agreement; retention under S1.10). Annex II is completed by S1.6, and Annex III by Schedule 2. Where Clause 13 applies, the competent supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia. Under Clauses 17 and 18 the Clauses are governed by Slovenian law and disputes under them are decided by the courts of Ljubljana, Slovenia, notwithstanding Agreement section 13.
(c) *Onward transfers.* For transfers to the providers in Schedule 2, MOTRES relies on an adequacy decision, including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider's entity is certified for the relevant service, or concludes the applicable module with the provider (Module 3 where MOTRES acts as processor, Module 2 where it acts as controller), in each case with the required transfer assessment and supplementary measures and before the affected transfer.
(d) *UK and Switzerland.* Where UK or Swiss law applies, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the necessary Swiss adaptations are completed for the affected transfer before it takes place; this general reference alone does not satisfy them.
(e) *Precedence and copies.* No exclusion, remedy limitation or dispute clause of the Agreement limits the liability, rights, remedies or jurisdiction provisions of the Clauses; the Clauses prevail in a conflict. Copies of the applicable safeguards are available at support@aerostator.com, subject to lawful redactions.

## Schedule 3 — Export Register

S3.1 **Self-service export (account menu, "Download my data").** A ZIP archive by a signed, single-use link: account record, consents and communication preferences, sessions, API and agent keys, authorisation grants, authentication and agent-tool audit records, support requests, usage records, administrator-access records, and the whole personal workspace and published folders, including machine configurations, operating points and saved simulation results, in their native YAML, JSON, JSONL and CSV files.

S3.2 **Export on request.** Development projects (specifications, threads, project files, reports and Results stored in projects), production records and anything not covered by S3.1 are exported on written request to support@aerostator.com, in their native formats (JSON, YAML, CSV, PDF, STEP and the uploaded file formats), within 30 days and, in a switching process, within the transitional period of Agreement section 11.7. The Client may also read its data through the Platform's API and agent tools. Every export carries a manifest that lists each included category, its schema version, the relationships between files and their formats, and each withheld item with its specific rights-based reason.

S3.3 **Excluded categories.** Model Materials supplied by MOTRES under the Model Access NDA and restricted MOTRES library content are withheld from exports and marked as withheld. MOTRES's internal model weights, internal training datasets and internal-only technology are excluded only to the extent they are not exportable data or digital assets of the Client or digital assets the Client has a right to use independently. Client-origin data are not withheld merely because they appear in a mixed record; MOTRES provides a usable separated copy where possible without disclosing protected provider or third-party material. These exclusions do not obstruct switching of the Client's own data.

S3.4 **Infrastructure and safeguards.** Infrastructure jurisdictions: Germany (EU) for the Platform's servers and databases; the EU for its backups; the EU, the United States and the countries of approved translation endpoints for the providers in Schedule 2. Against conflicting foreign-government access, MOTRES applies approval of staff and provider access, encryption of backups with keys held by MOTRES, validation and, where there are grounds, challenge of requests that lack a valid legal basis under EU law, disclosure of only what is required (Agreement section 5.4) and notice to the Client where legally permitted. The current jurisdictions and safeguards are published on the page linked beside this Schedule.

S3.5 Export, switching and egress are free of charge (Agreement section 11.6).

S3.6 The structures and field descriptions of the exported files, the open interface documentation, applicable standards and known limitations are published on the page linked beside this Schedule and kept up to date.
