## Main finding — the magnet

At the card's own maximum working temperature (150 °C) F52SH in this rotor demagnetises
at every current: the settled TDM points keep 93.6 % Br at 0.5·I0, 90.8 % at I0 and
85.8 % at the peak current; the rated operating torque is 0.85 × the virgin map (demag
share 0.853). The 99.5 %-retention knee is extrapolated to ~1.4 A. The cooling studies
put the magnet at its 150 °C limit first in every propeller case. So the owner decision
is not only "which temperature": either a higher-coercivity grade (UH/EH) or a magnet
temperature well below 150 °C is needed for 25.88 A — the sensitivity runs at 120 /
100 °C (below) show the rated point losing 0.98 % (100 °C) and 4.3 % (120 °C) of its torque to demag, against 14.7 % at 150 °C: the owner's ≤ 1 % rule holds at rated current only with the magnet at ≈ 100 °C or below.

## Coolings — how to read the two models

- **Coupled FEM** (`routes.coupled.run`, S1 = `coupled_continuous_rating`): robotics is the
  existing mode exactly as the rated duty saved it (still air 40 °C + radiation ε 0.9, heat
  path into the housing/mount, both end faces in still air, still bore); it is limited by the
  70 °C touch limit of the housing. Propeller air = the air mode at the slipstream speed of
  the G40×13.1 (motor behind the hub, factor 0.4): that mode cools the LATERAL housing only
  (no end faces, no bore) — on a 13 mm × Ø85 pancake that is under a quarter of the surface,
  so the FEM propeller rows are a lower bound; the magnet (rotor) limits them.
- **Configure lumped** (the web's `thermalEstimate`, ported): lateral housing + both end
  faces at one film; robotics = still air + radiation (no mount), propeller = cross-flow at
  the slipstream. These are the numbers Configure's temperature row and thermal zones show.
- The magnet reaches 150 °C first in every propeller case: at that temperature the rated
  point already loses 14.7 % torque to demag — see the main finding.
