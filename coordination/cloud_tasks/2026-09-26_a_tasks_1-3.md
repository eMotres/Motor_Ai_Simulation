## Задание 2 — Br «угол» и установление синусного напряжения

1. Demag: the worst-element Br is a sharp-corner value that never converges with mesh refinement
   (see `docs/NO_FILTERS_2026-09-24.md`). Keep the volume-weighted Br kept as the magnet number; report the
   worst element as a flagged "corner" diagnostic (location + value), not as the magnet's figure, in the result,
   web summary and report.
2. Sine-voltage drive settles for a fixed 4 periods by default (`f83e60f`), which leaves ~1 % on torque ripple
   (7.126 → 7.202 % at 40 periods). Replace the fixed count with a convergence criterion on the period-to-period
   change of the reported quantities (current amplitude, torque mean and ripple), with a cap, and record how many
   periods were used and the final residual. Show before/after on the fixtures.

## Задание 3 — постоянная составляющая при ШИМ и Stop

1. PWM voltage drive uses a "DC anchor" during settling that leaves 1.1–1.2 A of DC in the reported window on a
   small machine but is needed on a large one (43 A otherwise, hours to decay). Replace it with an exact
   solution of the steady DC offset (the periodic steady state has zero net DC for a symmetric bridge; solve the
   DC component consistently instead of anchoring it). See `docs/NO_FILTERS_2026-09-24.md`, item 5.
2. Stop/cancel: a cancel during the solver's setup before the first time step is not honoured (up to ~30 s
   delay). Add cancel checks in the pre-start phase of `src/motor_ai_sim/simulation/fem_solver_2d.py`
   (mesh, projections, initial static solve, demag pre-pass), with a test.
