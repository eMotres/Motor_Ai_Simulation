Subject: AeroStator: run motor simulations yourself, free of charge

Hello,

Until now, simulations on AeroStator were run for you by MOTRES engineers. From now on you run them yourself, free of charge: electromagnetic, thermal and mechanical FEM analysis, optimisation and the other design tools for permanent-magnet motors and generators.

## What you can do now

- **Simulate your own designs.** Change a design in Configure and start FEM jobs from your account.
- **Start a development project** with the specification and design assistants, and invite your engineers and manufacturers.
- **Bring your AI agent.** AeroStator speaks MCP (https://aerostator.com/mcp): your agent can browse the catalogue, check motor suitability and submit simulation jobs.

**Fair use:** one simulation runs at a time per account; further jobs wait in your queue and start when the previous one has finished.

The deal is stated up front: in FREE mode we train our own AI models on the engineering data of your projects, never on personal data, and your projects stay confidential. PRIVATE mode excludes all AI training. Before your first project or simulation you accept the [Platform Agreement](https://aerostator.com/platform-agreement); details are in the [Terms of Use](https://aerostator.com/terms).
