Subject: AeroStator: simulation is now free, and how we use engineering data

Hello,

All simulations on AeroStator are now free of charge for every account: electromagnetic, thermal and mechanical FEM analysis, optimisation and the other engineering tools, within the compute queues that apply to everyone.

## What you accept in return

To keep simulation free, we use engineering data from FREE mode to train our own AI models for motor design. Please read this before you continue:

- **What is used:** the engineering data of your projects and workspace, such as geometry, materials, settings, simulation results, optimisation histories and test data you enter, including data you entered before.
- **What is never used:** personal data. Names, e-mail addresses and company names are removed first.
- **Your projects stay closed.** We do not publish or sell your data or show it to other users. Training is not publication. Sharing your data in the community dataset remains a separate opt-in.
- **What stays with us:** models already trained before you switch to PRIVATE or delete data are kept and not retrained; we owe no payment, attribution or report for the training use.
- **No AI training at all:** PRIVATE mode, for companies, with a fee agreed in an order. A switch to PRIVATE also stops training on your earlier data.
- **"As is":** the free service comes without warranty and without liability, except where the law does not allow it to be excluded. Liability for a product arises only under a separate paid agreement.

The next time you sign in, AeroStator asks you to accept the updated Terms of Use and the Platform Agreement. Until you accept, your data are not used for AI training.
