**Stored-energy periodicity remains unproved.** The NPZ holds complete `A` and quadrature B, but not the solver's assembled per-element nonlinear B-H law/reluctivity assignment, magnet source vectors, or a defined total stored-energy functional including PM terms. The saved mesh tags alone cannot supply these, especially as magnet IDs permute. `fem_solver_2d.py` assembles `nu_base2`/`_sat2` and PM loads from its material objects before the frame loop; `p2_nonlinear.py` uses pointwise B-H quadrature in the accepted Newton path. A defensible energy comparison must use that same constitutive interpolation and quadrature, the mapped PM source and irreversible state, and a stated sign/constant convention. Reconstructing only `0.5 AᵀK(A)A`, or integrating the plotted triangle-mean B, would not be nonlinear stored energy. The next capture or read-only reconstruction should save the exact per-element material and PM-source data and validate its energy functional on a controlled case before using it for periodic port-work promotion.
