Evaluate the same stored-energy functional already reconstructed for run04/run06 (`∫H dB` at P2 quadrature, linear-region `½νB²`, PM term `−Hc·B`), including rotor material/tag permutation. Then compute terminal work with `periodic_terminal_flux_work_per_mechanical_radian` and compare its mean with independently evaluated frozen-current virtual work and raw Maxwell torque on the same states and an angular-refinement check. Keep disagreement visible; no candidate is selected by current amplitude.

Those measurements are useful closure diagnostics, but no validated tolerance turns them into exact periodicity, and they do not cover the entire trajectory. The public solver result has phase currents/linkages and angles but lacks a complete, provenance-bound periodic material-state certificate; `picard_converged`, matching endpoints, and a small even-sample coarsening change do not substitute for it. No production call to the terminal-work helper exists yet. Thus the immediate implementation unit is a **diagnostic-only certificate/capture and comparison test**, not a selector replacement. Its output should remain `uncertified` if the closure or independent torque agreement cannot be established.
